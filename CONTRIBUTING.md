# Contributing

Useful corrections and improvements are welcome.

Good contributions include:

- fixing ambiguous wording;
- adding missing security or AI-governance considerations;
- improving evidence mappings;
- clarifying ownership or review cadence;
- correcting formatting issues.

Please avoid:

- copying proprietary customer questionnaires;
- adding confidential information;
- reproducing licensed industry questionnaires verbatim;
- adding claims that imply certification or regulatory approval;
- SEO spam or promotional links.

For substantial changes, explain the rationale and the operational problem the change solves.
